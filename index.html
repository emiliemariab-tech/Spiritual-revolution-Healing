Meet your guide

Hi, I’m Emilie.

I’m Emilie Bouffard, founder of Spiritual Revolution Healing. My work brings together a lifelong interest in human behavior with studies in psychology, philosophy, anthropology, and classical music.

I have studied and practiced meditation since 2014, growing into my role as a meditation leader. Since 2017, I have also studied Astrology and Human Design in depth, bringing these systems into my work as thoughtful tools for self-knowledge, timing, and alignment.

I support individuals, couples, and leaders through personal and relational transitions. My approach is calm, practical, and deeply attentive to the person behind the problem. Together, we can create space for clearer choices and more honest connection.
